# Landing — public sales funnel

The public site at escuelasoberana.com. Its job is to inform visitors well enough
that only the genuinely interested ones reach Marina, and to give everyone else a
smaller next step rather than a dead end.

## Language

### Offers

**Máster Soberana**:
The flagship 6-month program: live online classes plus individual and group
accompaniment, sold on a 1:1 call rather than self-serve checkout.
_Avoid_: the course, the program (ambiguous — there is more than one)

**Tus Guías Te Hablan**:
The recorded low-ticket intro course (~100€), bought self-serve. The entry rung
below Máster Soberana. Currently hosted on the legacy site marinaromes.com.
_Avoid_: the intro, the mini-course

**Offer ladder**:
The two offers as a progression — Tus Guías Te Hablan leads to Máster Soberana.
A visitor who isn't ready for the máster is shown the rung below it, never a refusal.

### Audience

**Aspiring therapist**:
The avatar the page speaks to — someone who wants to take paying clients of their
own. This is *targeting*, not *eligibility*: it shapes the copy, and never gates
anyone out or changes where a lead ranks. Whether a visitor holds this intent is
noted for Marina's benefit on the call, nothing more.
_Avoid_: ideal client, target market

**Self-learner**:
A visitor who wants Registros Akáshicos for themselves, with no intention of
practising professionally. Fully welcome on the máster — earlier cohorts were mixed.
_Avoid_: hobbyist (reads as less serious than these students are), unqualified lead

### Scarcity

**Rolling enrolment**:
How Máster Soberana actually runs: a student's 6 months begins when she enrols,
not on a date shared with anyone else. Two alumnas can be in month 1 and month 5
of the same máster at the same time, and nobody can arrive too late.
_Avoid_: cohort, grupo de septiembre, intake, promoción (all imply a shared start
date the máster does not have)

**Capacity**:
The most alumnas Marina can accompany at once, set by the weekly individual
follow-up she owes each one. This is the máster's real scarcity — it is
programme-wide, and it is true every time it is stated.
_Avoid_: plazas del grupo, últimas plazas (both describe a group that doesn't exist)

**Launch offer**:
The one-time promotion marking the platform's first opening: extra individual
sessions for enrolling before a stated date. Its urgency is credible precisely
because it does not recur — once it closes, it is gone rather than renewed.
_Avoid_: cuenta atrás, oferta de 3 días

### The funnel

**Qualification**:
Deciding, from a visitor's own answers, which offer to put in front of her. It
rests only on facts she supplies about time, money and schedule — never on a
judgement about who she is. She is routed rather than refused, and the reason is
quoted back from what she just answered. She is never shown a score, a verdict,
or a door that closed.
_Avoid_: screening, vetting, gating, rejection

**Qualified lead**:
A visitor who can give the máster the time, the money and the schedule it asks
for, and is therefore worth Marina's time on a 1:1 call. A description of
circumstances, not a status the visitor holds or is told about.

**Deflection**:
The page's core job: reducing the number of unqualified information requests
reaching Marina. Achieved by disclosure — duration and commitment stated plainly
on the page, price disclosed inside the qualifier rather than in the sales copy —
so visitors select themselves out, never by the system rejecting them.
_Avoid_: filtering out, disqualifying

**Newsletter**:
Marina's ongoing email list. Mostly orbits Máster Soberana but also shares her
own work and thinking, and sometimes sells nothing at all. Signing up promises
her letters — not a notification when enrolment opens.
_Avoid_: waitlist, "avísame de la próxima apertura" (both promise a dated opening
we haven't committed to)
